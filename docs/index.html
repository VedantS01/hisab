Hisab (हिसाब)

Private, on-device expense analysis for Indian statements.

Get it on the App Store Source on GitHub

Import your Google Pay, Paytm, or BHIM transaction exports and your HDFC / IDFC FIRST bank statements. Hisab parses them deterministically, organizes everything into monthly buckets, cross-checks payment-app records against bank statements, and shows you where the money went — entirely on your iPhone.

  • Nine statement formats, each verified to the paisa against the statement's own printed totals.
  • Reconciliation: every app payment is matched to its bank-statement row by UPI reference; unmatched payments are flagged.
  • No duplicates, ever: overlapping and re-imported statements collapse by transaction identity.
  • Open source (Apache-2.0) — add a parser for your own bank.

Privacy Policy

Effective with Hisab 2.0, November 2026.

Hisab is designed so that the developer never has your data:

  • No data collection. Hisab collects and shares no data — financial or otherwise. There are no analytics, no trackers, no crash reporting, no advertising identifiers.
  • Your ledger stays on your device. Statements you import, alerts Hisab captures, and the categories you set are parsed and stored only in the app's private storage on your device.
  • Statements are read on your phone. Reading an unfamiliar statement's columns uses Apple's language model on iPhone (Google's Gemini Nano on Android), on the device. Your statement rows never leave it.
  • AI features send only what you ask about. "Find in text" sends the text you pasted, and monthly insights send that month's category totals, recurring payments and merchant names, to Hisab's gateway (a Cloudflare Worker run by the developer) and on to the model provider that answers (currently Groq; Google's Gemini as a fallback). The gateway keeps no copy of the text and no account: each install holds an anonymous token and a credit balance. The provider processes the request under its API terms and does not train on it. Nothing is sent unless you use these features; on iPhone, when the gateway is unreachable or out of credits, Apple's on-device model answers instead.
  • Home-screen layout. Hisab downloads the layout of its home screen, a public file, from this website (GitHub Pages). It sends nothing about you; like any website, GitHub sees the request's IP address.
  • No accounts. There is nothing to sign up for and no server of the developer's to hold your information.
  • Statement passwords you choose to save are stored in the iOS Keychain on your device only.
  • Deleting data: remove individual documents in the app, use Settings → Erase all data, or delete the app — nothing persists anywhere else.

Because the developer never receives your data, the developer cannot access, recover, sell, or delete it on your behalf. This policy can only change alongside the app's open-source code, in public.
