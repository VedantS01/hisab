## Description

Hisab (हिसाब) turns the statements you already download into a clear
picture of your money — without your data ever leaving your phone.

Import transaction exports from Google Pay, Paytm, or BHIM, and bank
statements from HDFC Bank or IDFC FIRST Bank (PDF, XLSX, XLS, or TXT —
password-protected PDFs supported). Hisab parses them deterministically
and builds your personal bahi-khata:

• MONTHLY BUCKETS — every statement lights up the months it covers; a
yearly statement fills twelve at once. See at a glance which months
still await which statement.

• RECONCILIATION — every app payment is matched against your bank
statement by UPI reference. Instantly see which payments the bank
confirms, which are missing, and what you spent outside the apps.

• NO DOUBLE COUNTING — re-import a statement, import overlapping
periods, or the same statement in two formats: transaction identity
guarantees zero duplicates. Bank rows matched to app payments count
once. Transfers between your own accounts are excluded automatically.

• ANALYTICS — monthly spend, income and net, six-month trend, category
breakdown with editable rules, top merchants. Amounts in proper Indian
notation (₹1,23,456.78).

• PRIVATE BY ARCHITECTURE — no account, no network access, no
analytics, no tracking. Your financial data stays in the app's private
storage on your device. The entire app is open source (Apache-2.0).

Made for India's statement formats. Missing your bank? The parser
framework is open — contribute one on GitHub.

## Notes for App Review

Hisab analyses statement files the user already possesses; it has no
login and makes no network requests. To evaluate every screen without
Indian bank statements: Settings → "Load demo data" fills three months
of synthetic statements (dashboard, buckets grid, transactions, and the
reconciliation screen all populate). "Erase all data" resets. Bank and
payment-app names in the UI identify statement formats the app can
read; no affiliation is claimed and no brand logos are used.
